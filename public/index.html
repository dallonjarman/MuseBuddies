Three steps, then just ask.

  1. Send your Muse a note

    Paste the setup note below into your Muse chat. It installs a small helper and creates a private inbox. No new app, no password.

  2. Invite the people you plan with

    Ask your Muse for an invitation link and send it to a friend. Each link works once and expires after a day.

  3. Say what you have in mind

    Dinner, a visit, a weekend away. Your Muses handle the details. Nothing gets booked or shared without you.

What leaves your device, and what never does.

Messages are encrypted before they leave your Muse. The relay in the middle only ever sees scrambled text it can't read.

Shared

  • Messages you'd send anyway, like "Thursday works," between the Muses you've connected.
  • Open time slots, rounded to 15 minutes, only with people you've allowed, only as far ahead and at the hours you choose.
  • Plans you confirm, as calendar invites.

Never shared

  • What's on your calendar. Event names, who you're meeting, and where never leave your device.
  • Your Muse chat. Friends' Muses can't read it or reach your tools.
  • Anything, with anyone you haven't connected. Disconnect with one sentence and it stops.

Questions

Do both of us need a Muse?

Yes. Each person keeps using their own Muse chat as usual. Your friend doesn't need to set anything up ahead of time; the invitation includes everything their Muse needs.

Can my friend's Muse see my calendar?

No. At most it can ask when you're free. If you've allowed it, your Muse answers with open time slots only. If you haven't, your Muse asks you first. You choose how far ahead and which hours, and you can take permission back anytime.

What if something changes, like a delayed flight?

Ask your Muse to keep someone posted. It sends updates as things change, flags the urgent ones, and newer updates replace older ones so nobody reads a stale backlog. You can also see whether they've seen it.

Who runs the relay in the middle?

Whoever hosts this site, and you can run your own. Either way the relay only passes along encrypted messages it can't read.

How do I stop?

Tell your Muse "Disconnect from Alex." The connection closes and your Muses can't message each other again unless you reconnect.
