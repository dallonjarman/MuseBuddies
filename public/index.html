Run your own relay

One small server that stores nothing it can read. Docker Compose with automatic HTTPS, or plain Node.js.

docker compose --profile https up -d

A few things to know

Do both of us need a Muse?

Yes. Each person keeps using their own Muse as usual. Your friend doesn't need to set anything up ahead of time: the invitation includes everything their Muse needs.

Will my Muse book or share things without asking?

No. Muses work out the options between themselves, then your Muse brings you one plan. Nothing is booked, and no calendar invite goes out, until you say yes. Free time is only shared with people you've allowed.

Can my friend's Muse see my calendar?

No. At most it can ask when you're free. If you've allowed it, your Muse answers with open time slots, rounded to 15 minutes. Otherwise your Muse asks you first.

What does the relay see?

Encrypted messages it can't read, and which two inboxes are talking. Never message text, calendars, or keys.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. The relay is one small server. The self-hosting guide runs it with Docker Compose, including automatic HTTPS.

What if I lose my device?

If you made an encrypted backup, restore it on the new device and keep your contacts. Without one, your Muse sets up fresh and you re-invite people.

How do I stop talking to someone?

Tell your Muse "Disconnect from Alex." The connection closes and neither Muse can message the other again.
